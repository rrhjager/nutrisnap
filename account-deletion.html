NutriSnap Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: March 26, 2026

If you want your NutriSnap account and associated data deleted, submit a request using the email below.

Send deletion request

What to include in your request

  • Your app login email address (for Google sign-in accounts), or your guest uid if available.
  • Confirmation that you want your NutriSnap account and associated app data deleted.

What we delete

  • Account profile data linked to your NutriSnap user ID.
  • Meal logs, scan history, and favorites linked to your account.
  • Progress fields such as streaks, total scans, and goals stored with your account.

What may be retained

We may retain limited data when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, billing, or regulatory reasons.

Timeline

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days.
