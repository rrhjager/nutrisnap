NutriSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data NutriSnap processes, why it is processed, where it is stored, and your rights under the GDPR.

1. Data Controller

NutriSnap
Contact: kaartenautomaat@gmail.com

2. Data We Process

GuestGoogle Sign-InPremium

  • Profile data: uid, displayName, email (for Google sign-in), photoURL, isGuest, isPremium.
  • Tracking/progress: scansToday, lastScanDate, totalScans, currentStreak, longestStreak, unlockedBadges.
  • Settings/goals: dailyCalorieGoal, dailyProteinGoal, dailyCarbsGoal, dailyFatGoal, waterAmount, hasCompletedOnboarding.
  • Log data in Firestore (`users/{uid}/meals`, `users/{uid}/scans`, `users/{uid}/favorites`).
  • Premium-only: isPremium, premiumSource, Google Play subscription reference fields, stripeCustomerId, stripeSubscriptionId, and premium update timestamps.

3. Local Device Storage

  • `darkMode`
  • `guest_scan_count` and `guest_scan_date` (guest flow)
  • `googleFitToken` in sessionStorage (active session only)

4. Camera, Barcode, and Voice

For food analysis, we process camera images, barcode input, and optional voice/text meal descriptions. This data is used to calculate nutritional values such as food name, calories, protein, carbs, fat, serving size, ingredients, and confidence.

5. Health Connect Data

Premium users can optionally connect Android Health Connect. If enabled, NutriSnap requests read access only for active calories burned so the Logbook can show burned kcal and net kcal for the day.

NutriSnap does not write data to Health Connect. Health Connect data is not used for advertising and can be revoked in Android Health Connect settings at any time.

6. Why We Use Data

  • Authentication and account management
  • Food analysis and meal logging features
  • Scan limits, goals, progress, and badges
  • Premium subscription management
  • Optional Google Fit synchronization
  • Optional Health Connect burned-calorie display for Premium users
  • Android local reminder notifications

7. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Auth + Firestore)
  • Google Gemini API (AI analysis)
  • Open Food Facts (barcode product lookup)
  • Google Play Billing (Android subscriptions)
  • Stripe (legacy web checkout flows)
  • Google Fit API (optional for connected Google accounts)
  • Android Health Connect (optional active calories burned display for Premium users)
  • Hosting platform (server/API processing)

8. Payments

NutriSnap does not store full payment card numbers. Android app payments are handled through Google Play Billing. Stripe may still be used for legacy web checkout flows. NutriSnap stores only subscription reference data (for example customer/subscription IDs).

9. Legal Bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of our service, including account, app features, and subscription functionality.
  • Consent, for example camera access, notifications, Google Fit, or Health Connect connection.
  • Legitimate interests, including security, debugging, and abuse prevention.

10. Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active or until deletion is requested. Guest data is intended for temporary use but may remain until removed. Health Connect access can be revoked in Android settings. NutriSnap reads Health Connect data on demand for the daily overview.

11. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restriction/objection
  • Right to data portability (where feasible)
