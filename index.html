NutriSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data NutriSnap processes, why it is processed, and your rights under the GDPR.

1. Data Controller

NutriSnap
Contact: kaartenautomaat@gmail.com

2. Data We Process

GuestGoogle Sign-InPremium

  • Profile data: uid, displayName, email (for Google sign-in), photoURL, isGuest, isPremium.
  • Tracking/progress: scansToday, lastScanDate, totalScans, currentStreak, longestStreak, unlockedBadges.
  • Settings/goals: dailyCalorieGoal, dailyProteinGoal, dailyCarbsGoal, dailyFatGoal, waterAmount, hasCompletedOnboarding.
  • Log data in Firestore (`users/{uid}/meals`, `users/{uid}/scans`, `users/{uid}/favorites`).
  • Premium-only: stripeCustomerId, stripeSubscriptionId, updatedAt.

3. Local Device Storage

  • `darkMode`
  • `guest_scan_count` and `guest_scan_date` (guest flow)
  • `googleFitToken` in sessionStorage (active session only)

4. Camera, Barcode, and Voice

For food analysis, we process camera images, barcode input, and optional voice/text meal descriptions. This data is used to calculate nutritional values.

5. Why We Use Data

  • Authentication and account management
  • Food analysis and meal logging features
  • Scan limits, goals, progress, and badges
  • Premium subscription management
  • Optional Google Fit synchronization
  • Android local reminder notifications

6. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Auth + Firestore)
  • Google Gemini API (AI analysis)
  • Open Food Facts (barcode product lookup)
  • Stripe (payments and subscriptions)
  • Google Fit API (optional for connected Google accounts)
  • Hosting platform (server/API processing)

7. Payments

NutriSnap does not store full card numbers. Payments are handled through Stripe Checkout. NutriSnap stores only subscription reference data (for example customer/subscription IDs).

8. Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active or until deletion is requested. Guest data is intended for temporary use but may remain until removed.

9. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restriction/objection
  • Right to data portability (where feasible)

Privacy requests: kaartenautomaat@gmail.com

10. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always published on this page.
